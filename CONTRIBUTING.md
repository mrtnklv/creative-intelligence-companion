# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in The Creative Intelligence Companion.

The project is at an early stage. Contributions that clarify the problem, improve the prototype, test assumptions or strengthen privacy and accessibility are welcome.

## Before contributing
- Read the README, Product Vision and Architecture documents.
- Check existing issues and discussions before starting substantial work.
- For a large change, open an issue first to discuss the problem and proposed direction.
- Remember that the architecture is provisional and should remain as simple as possible.

## Ways to contribute
- Report bugs and usability problems.
- Improve documentation and clarify requirements.
- Evaluate audio capture and speech-to-text options.
- Contribute code, tests and accessibility improvements.
- Test the experience in real meeting environments.
- Review privacy, security and data-retention assumptions.
- Propose integrations that fit the open and modular direction.

## Pull requests
Please aim for focused pull requests that:
- Explain the problem and proposed solution.
- Include relevant tests or a clear manual test plan.
- Update documentation when behavior or setup changes.
- Avoid committing credentials, private recordings or personal data.
- Preserve the consent-first and quiet-by-default principles.

Do not submit recordings or transcripts from real people unless everyone involved has explicitly agreed to their use for development and sharing.

## Design principles
- The assistant must not initiate conversation or interrupt without being invoked.
- Recording must be explicit, visible and consent-based.
- AI-generated interpretations must not be presented as confirmed participant decisions.
- Research responses should preserve source references.
- Provider-specific integrations should remain replaceable where practical.
- Human review and correction should remain possible.

## Conduct
Be respectful, constructive and open to different disciplines and perspectives. Critique ideas and implementations without attacking contributors.

## Project maturity
The project is experimental. Interfaces, architecture and scope may change as the prototype is tested. Please discuss major changes before investing significant work.
